Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email support@famio.cc and we'll reply within two working days.

I logged something with no signal. Is it lost?
No. Banda saves every entry on your phone first and sends it when you're back online. If the server ever refuses an entry, the Farm tab shows "Fix entries that didn't save" so you can edit or remove it.
How do I add a worker?
Open the Farm tab and create an invite. Give the worker the one-time code. They sign up on their own phone, then tap "Join with a code" and enter it.
I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen tap "Forgot password?". We email you a code to set a new one.
Why didn't I get a vaccine reminder?
Reminders need notifications turned on for Banda in iPhone Settings → Notifications.
How do I delete my account?
Farm tab → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and every record your farm owns. If you're a worker on someone else's farm, you leave the farm and the owner keeps their records.
