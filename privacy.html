Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Banda is a record-keeping app for broiler farmers, made by Samuel Mukabi ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: we collect only what the app needs to keep your farm records, we never sell it, and there are no ads or tracking.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and passwordTo create your account and sign you in. Passwords are stored hashed; we can't read them.
Your name (optional)Shown to people on your farm so they know who logged what.
Farm records: batches, daily logs, deaths, feed, weights, vaccines, medicines, sales and invitesThis is what the app is for. It lets you see your records on any phone and share them with your team.
Account IDTo link your records to your account.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, health data, payment details, browsing history, advertising identifiers or analytics about how you use the app.

Where it's stored

Records are kept on your phone so the app works offline, and in a database hosted by Supabase, our infrastructure provider, which processes the data only on our behalf. Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Who can see it

Only you and the people you invite to your farm. Workers you invite can see and add to your farm's records; you can remove them at any time. We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising. We'd only disclose it if the law required us to.

Notifications

Vaccine and feed reminders are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in iPhone Settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your records for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time from the Farm tab → Delete account. That permanently removes your account and every record your farm owns from our servers. If you're a worker on someone else's farm, deleting your account removes you from that farm; the owner keeps the records they own. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or to have it deleted.

Children

Banda is meant for farmers and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update this page and the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.

Contact

Email support@famio.cc with any questions about your privacy.
